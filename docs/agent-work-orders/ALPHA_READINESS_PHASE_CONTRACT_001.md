# ALPHA READINESS PHASE CONTRACT 001

## Authority and boundary

This work order governs `ALPHA-READY-001 — Alpha Readiness Foundation` after
canonical Gate-7 completion. `AGENTS.md`, canonical Product governance, and
`DEC-PROD-058` remain controlling. Only one write-bearing task may be active.
No Baseline A freeze, benchmark execution, cognition optimization, Alpha
Operations, launch, push, deployment, release, promotion, production access,
or customer-state access is authorized.

Alpha Readiness reuses existing authorization, current-access, Source Content,
Evidence, Explanation, Organizational Understanding, Product Artifact,
Product Workflow, replay, CAS, and recovery owners. Readiness evidence is
noncanonical and cannot authorize, mutate Product truth, or influence cognition.

## Accepted decisions

1. A successfully reviewed and closed occurrence is durable,
   organization-scoped user-created material only when immutable Review and
   closure receipts bind the exact organization, Product Question, meeting
   series, occurrence, user, and authorization state. It remains governed by
   its own current authorization. Upstream revocation does not erase accepted
   closure content; inaccessible upstream content, cognition, confidence, or
   provenance remains unavailable and cannot influence later output.
2. An occurrence may Freeze, Capture, Review, and close with an exact empty
   Private Working contribution set. No synthetic contribution or reference is
   permitted. Capture notes remain independent of Private Working contribution
   and Capture may be explicitly empty where existing Product behavior permits.
   Review explicitly accepts the empty or notes-only outcome, closure references
   the real Freeze, Capture, Review, and closure receipts, and reload remains
   truthful.
3. Alpha observability uses a dedicated noncanonical, closed, versioned,
   content-free schema with injected sinks and no free-form or raw identity,
   credential, body, excerpt, note, Private Working, or derived-cognition data.
4. Alpha telemetry is durable, opt-in, disabled by default, provider-neutral,
   and enabled only with written design-partner organization consent plus a
   clear in-product notice. Ordinary lifecycle events require no per-event
   prompt after consent; voluntary usefulness feedback remains separately
   optional for usefulness, surprise reduction, and understanding improvement.
   Telemetry is pseudonymized with an environment-managed key, uses a closed
   dimension set covering event type, workflow stage, outcome category,
   occurrence number, role and viewport categories, latency bucket,
   Product/build identity, progressive-disclosure category, and voluntary-
   response category, has mandatory automatic expiry after 90 days by default, organization-scoped and
   independently deletable within seven days, and accessible only to explicitly
   designated Alpha operators through auditable access. Retained aggregates may
   not permit reidentification, and telemetry access grants no Product, source,
   or cognition access. Telemetry cannot affect Product truth, cognition,
   authorization, or recommendations. Production sinks are deferred.
5. Failed cleanup blocks readiness until temporary acceptance users, sessions,
   memberships, organizations, browser profiles, server processes, test roots,
   protected temporary files, and other externally created acceptance state are
   removed and independently verified absent. Governed retained readiness
   evidence and approved screenshots are not cleanup residue.
6. Production Runtime rollback remains owned by `ALPHA-OPS-001`; readiness
   validates only local and repository-backed non-destructive recovery and may
   document production rollback prerequisites without executing rollback.

## Execution contract

Routine work may proceed through bounded implementation, validation,
independent review, local commit, clean local fast-forward, and Control Tower
progression under the Routine Governed Lane. Every Sensitive subgate requires
one exact human Gate Contract specifying:

- objective and exact canonical parent;
- exact worktree, branch, and maximum path list with one responsibility each;
- exact canonical owners reused and trust/privacy decisions;
- expected diff categories, acceptance criteria, and negative controls;
- independent-review plan and stopping conditions; and
- requested local commit and clean-fast-forward authority.

Once approved, that exact Sensitive Gate Contract covers implementation through
local canonical integration without separate routine commit or integration
requests, unless scope or repository state drifts or fast-forward ceases to be
clean.

Every subgate stops for a canonical Product or architecture change, new
unapproved owner or persistence boundary, identity/authorization source,
migration/backfill, unresolved privacy/security judgment, production access,
protected-value leakage, cleanup residue, or mandatory failure that cannot be
remediated inside its approved scope.
