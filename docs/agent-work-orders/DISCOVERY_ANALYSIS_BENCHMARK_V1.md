# Discovery Analysis Benchmark V1

Identity: `discovery-analysis-benchmark-v1`

Status: development-only product research. This benchmark and every analysis it contains are noncanonical. It does not change the live Product, organizational history, Runtime, authorization, or production state.

## Method

The controlling sequence is:

`source packet → independent system analysis → deterministic hard gates → blind human review → immutable result`

Every comparison requires exact source equivalence. Native Discovery and provider outputs remain immutable and separate from content-safe comparison projections. Discovery never receives frontier output, preferred answers, human scores, or comparison results as analysis input.

Hard gates and human preference answer different questions. A structured, semantic, citation, authorization, or provenance hard-gate failure is ineligible for preference scoring. When both analyses pass, a human reviews neutral projections before reveal. Analysis quality and platform value remain separate; no combined automated score or model judge exists.

## Baseline and retry policy

An admitted case is immutable. Runs are not retried until preferred, and ambiguous provider dispatch remains ambiguous. Provider-entry accounting includes every preserved attempt. Rejected provider output is retained unchanged and may be locally re-adjudicated only when a deterministic validator defect is proven against the exact preserved bytes. Infrastructure-unresolved output is never converted into analytical evidence.

Source identity and evaluation-tooling identity are distinct. Analysis results remain bound to their original analysis-producing bytes; later presentation, validation, or packet tooling is recorded separately.

## Prohibited adaptation

- no frontier-output ingestion by Discovery;
- no exact-answer copying or preferred-answer source fixture;
- no benchmark-specific production branch;
- no repeated request omitted from accounting;
- no automated model judge;
- no Product or organizational-history write;
- no optimization of Northstar M1.

## Scenario-family result model

Each case records source packet, native runs, validation results, source equivalence, human-review state when eligible, provider accounting, and immutable packets. Scenario-family conclusions must distinguish human wins, hard-gate wins, and infrastructure-unresolved results.

## Northstar checkpoint

Discovery Quality Iteration 1 reached practical parity with the frozen frontier model on the Northstar scenario family. Discovery received the human preference on the initial M1 analysis, the frontier received the human preference when the principal audit evidence was removed, and Discovery preserved stricter commitment and chronology lineage in the temporal and contradiction cases where the frontier output failed the same due-date support hard gate.

This is one synthetic scenario family. The two frontier hard-gate losses share one repeated failure class and are not independent failure categories. No broad multi-domain superiority, production readiness, live Alpha integration, or improvement to the fixture-bound Product is established.

## Future acceptance

The multi-domain gate is provisional. A future candidate must be frozen before evaluation, run independently on multiple development domains, retain blank human scores until outputs exist, and then pass a separately governed sealed holdout created outside the optimization loop. Acceptance requires exact source equivalence, deterministic hard gates, immutable packets, human review where eligible, complete provider accounting, and no production integration by inference.

## Source and evidence sequencing

The 26-path implementation is reviewed and committed first. Benchmark evidence is then finalized against that immutable commit and committed separately. Benchmark analyses were produced under the frozen benchmark analysis identity. The later research-checkpoint implementation restores canonical interactive Product copy and accessibility IDs without changing the reviewed Studio preview outputs or analysis artifacts.
