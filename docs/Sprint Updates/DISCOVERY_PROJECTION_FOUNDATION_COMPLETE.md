# Discovery Projection Foundation Complete

## Status

The reusable Organizational Understanding Projection compiler and the inactive
Your Organization compatibility adapter are implemented and shadow validated.
The active product path remains on the Phase 8A Runtime-backed adapter.
Activation remains blocked by bounded communication/priority and
disclosure-decision dependencies.

This foundation is committed by the completion of this audit sprint.

## Purpose

Discovery now has a reusable bridge between governed canonical cognition and
product experience contracts. The bridge preserves organizational truth and
Governance boundaries while allowing each experience to shape already
disclosed information without traversing Runtime or recreating cognition.

The governing rule is:

> Projection selects, resolves, and reshapes disclosed canonical information.
> It does not infer, authorize, narrate, or own organizational truth.

## Architecture

```text
Persisted Runtime
        ↓
Canonical cognition
        ↓
Authority-qualified compositions
        ↓
Resolved disclosure decision
        ↓
Disclosure enforcement
        ↓
Organizational Understanding Projection
        ↓
Experience compatibility adapter
        ↓
Experience view model
        ↓
UI
```

Readable communication remains a separate branch:

```text
Disclosure-eligible cognition
        ↓
Application or understanding communication synthesis
        ↓
Communication projection
        ↓
Product communication
```

The shared Projection Layer must not synthesize human-readable narrative,
choose a primary Explanation, calculate confidence, or create recommendations.

## Completed capabilities

- reusable, versioned projection contracts;
- pure, deterministic, non-persistent compilation;
- an explicit resolved disclosure-result requirement;
- organization and consumer isolation;
- fail-closed withheld, revoked, mismatch, authority, reference, and
  historical states;
- canonical composition, revision, Explanation, uncertainty, Evidence-role,
  and supporting-reference closure;
- presentation-neutral progressive-disclosure structure;
- a thin projection-to-Your-Organization compatibility adapter;
- explicit compatibility availability and trace metadata;
- field-by-field semantic parity analysis;
- adapter-local rollback;
- deterministic validation and DEPS progression evidence.

The compiler does not access Runtime, and the compatibility adapter accepts
only completed projection output. Neither has a fixture, benchmark,
assessment-derived, or Runtime fallback. Neither is imported by the active
workspace builder.

## Intentionally unavailable

The foundation does not provide:

- canonical Explanation headlines or summaries;
- top or primary Explanation priority;
- displayable Evidence bodies;
- readable Understanding evolution;
- readable Theory evolution;
- audience-specific narrative;
- “why this matters” synthesis;
- active consumer-specific disclosure decisions.

These absences are explicit contract boundaries, not invitations for the
projection or compatibility adapter to invent content.

## Activation blockers

### Product Communication and Priority Contract

A valid downstream owner must provide:

- readable synthesis;
- audience framing;
- product-safe priority;
- “why this matters” language;
- change summaries;
- next-inquiry framing.

It must preserve canonical identity, uncertainty, unresolved alternatives,
and disclosure boundaries. It must not rewrite canonical Understanding.

### Disclosure-decision producer

A real producer must provide:

- organization identity;
- consumer identity;
- organizational scope;
- exact canonical revision references;
- authority-receipt validation;
- validity and supersession semantics;
- revocation semantics;
- fail-closed handling.

Current membership and permission infrastructure may supply inputs, but it is
not established as the canonical producer.

## Rollback

Rollback remains local to the projection and experience-adapter boundary.
Removing the compiler, compatibility adapter, validators, optional metadata,
documentation, and DEPS evidence requires no:

- Runtime migration;
- schema rollback;
- canonical rewriting;
- cognition change;
- disclosure-history mutation.

The active Phase 8A route requires no rollback because it was never switched.
