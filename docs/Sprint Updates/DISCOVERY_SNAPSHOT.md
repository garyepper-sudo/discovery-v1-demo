## Product identity

Discovery is a shared organizational intelligence platform. It exists to
maximize useful organizational understanding for any authorized person, team,
function, initiative, or organizational scope.

```text
Shared Organizational Intelligence
→ Organization Runtime
→ Organization Model
→ Operating Model and other representations
→ Applications and projections
```

- Organization Runtime is the canonical technical persistence boundary.
- The Organization Model is the living product representation.
- The Operating Model is the primary operational representation.
- Organizational Understanding is reusable platform cognition.
- Executive Assessment and the Executive Cognitive Operating System are
  application-specific consumers, not universal truth owners.

## Discovery 2 architecture

Adaptive Organizational Cognition is the organizing theory. Structured
Organizational Reasoning remains the technical execution architecture.

Discovery distinguishes:

- Evidence ancestry from comparative evidential role;
- persistence from cognitive authority;
- cognitive validity from disclosure eligibility;
- benchmark evidence from production capability;
- application projection from canonical cognition.

AI may contribute through bounded contracts. Discovery retains ownership of
identity, ancestry, contradiction, authority, retention, revision,
governance relationships, and organizational truth.
