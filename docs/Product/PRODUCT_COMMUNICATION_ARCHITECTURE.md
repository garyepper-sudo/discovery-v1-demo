# Discovery Product Communication Architecture

**Status:** Canonical product-layer architecture
**Authority:** Product communication only; not cognitive authority

## Purpose

Discovery exposes useful organizational understanding, not its internal
cognitive object graph. Canonical cognition remains the governed source of
organizational authority. Product communication deterministically translates
that cognition and admitted Evidence into language a user can understand and
act on without fabricating certainty, causality, or authority.

The canonical boundary is:

```text
User question
→ governed Evidence acquisition
→ canonical cognition
→ Product Translation
→ Product Understanding
→ Truthful Utility
→ user-facing experience
```

## Question-first product

Discovery begins with:

> What are you trying to understand?

The first-use objective is not to configure an organization, build an
Operating Model, or upload documents. Discovery establishes the user's
question first, then asks for the smallest useful additional Evidence.

The Organization Model and Operating Model remain important internal
capabilities and product representations. They are not onboarding assignments
for the user.

## Product Translation

Product Translation is a deterministic, non-authoritative projection between
canonical cognition and the user experience. It:

- consumes canonical artifacts and admitted Evidence read-only;
- preserves organization scope, lineage, and confidence;
- creates no cognitive authority;
- cannot complete an Explanation, revise a Condition, or fabricate cognition;
- expresses internal meaning in bounded product language.

Product Translation must not expose Mechanisms, Beliefs, Theories, Conditions,
or other internal cognitive vocabulary in the ordinary product experience.
A future expert or debugging mode may expose those objects intentionally, but
must remain separately authorized and clearly identified.

## Product Understanding

User-facing understanding has exactly three epistemic states:

- **Supported** — completed canonical authority supports the communication.
- **Provisional** — admitted Evidence supports a bounded, useful
  interpretation, but no new canonical authority is created.
- **Insufficient** — Evidence is genuinely insufficient for useful truthful
  communication.

Provisional is not a weaker synonym for Supported. It is a product
communication state with explicit limits. Insufficient must not be used merely
because canonical cognition is incomplete when a truthful bounded
interpretation is available.

## Maximum Truthful Utility

Discovery optimizes for **Maximum Truthful Utility**, not maximum certainty or
maximum completion.

Utility and confidence are independent:

- useful communication may have limited or unavailable confidence;
- high confidence does not guarantee useful communication;
- the product may communicate a bounded implication without asserting a
  recommendation or causal conclusion;
- uncertainty and missing Evidence remain visible.

Truthful Utility selects the most useful communication already supported. It
does not lower thresholds, change confidence, create cognition, or grant
authority.

## Product-owned Evidence Roles

Evidence Roles are canonical product-layer architecture and explicitly are not
canonical cognition. They deterministically classify the semantic purpose of
admitted canonical Evidence for Product Translation and Truthful Utility.

Evidence Roles:

- remain owned by the product communication layer;
- preserve exact Evidence and Observation lineage;
- preserve canonical confidence without recalculation;
- remain organization-isolated and deterministically ordered;
- reject questions, hypotheses, negations, causal assertions, and irrelevant
  lookalikes;
- create no Observations, Signals, Mechanisms, Conditions, Assessments, or
  Runtime state.

Product Evidence Roles are also distinct from comparative evidential roles in
Explanation adjudication. Product roles support communication; comparative
roles record how Evidence bears on competing explanations.

## Lineage and truthfulness

Every finding, contrast, implication, and Evidence basis must retain lineage to
the admitted canonical artifacts that support it. Product language may
compress or translate meaning, but it must not:

- add unsupported causal claims;
- turn alternatives into conclusions;
- convert provisional communication into canonical truth;
- invent quantitative values;
- cross organization boundaries;
- hide confidence limitations.

## Organizational Functions research boundary

Organizational Functions Experiment 001 is complete. It remains benchmark-only,
non-authoritative, and non-production.

The current research recommendation is Option 2: evaluate Organizational
Functions as a reusable non-authoritative interpretation layer. This is not a
promotion decision and does not imply future promotion.

The shadow treatment achieved:

- exact function recovery: 5/5;
- independent paraphrase recovery: 6/6;
- cross-industry transfer: 8/8;
- function precision and recall: 1.000;
- direction precision: 0.947;
- negative, negation, hypothesis, quotation, ambiguity, isolation, and
  determinism controls: pass.

Canonical promotion remains blocked pending:

- independent benchmark replication;
- a clear canonical producer and owner;
- persistence and migration strategies;
- low overlap with existing Signals, Phenomena, and Conditions;
- proof that simpler alternatives are insufficient;
- demonstrated value in independent consumers.

The next recommended research consumer is Evidence acquisition. It must remain
shadow-only until independently validated.

## Ownership rule

```text
Canonical cognition owns organizational authority.
Product communication owns truthful user utility.
Research owns no production authority.
```

No product communication object may flow back into canonical cognition unless
a separately reviewed contribution-validation contract explicitly authorizes
that transition.
