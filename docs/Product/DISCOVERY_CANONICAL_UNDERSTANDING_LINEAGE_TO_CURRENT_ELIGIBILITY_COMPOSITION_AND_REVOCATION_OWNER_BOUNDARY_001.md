## Ownership and eligibility

Immutable ancestry owns the historical material Evidence, admission, Source
Binding, support-role, ancestor-revision, and lineage-policy facts used to form
Theory and Explanation revisions. Existing scoped governance and Source Binding
owners retain current subject, organization, purpose, scope, sensitivity,
temporal, availability, and revocation authority. The canonical current-
eligibility resolver composes those owners into one read-only version-1 result;
the scoped authorized projection owns inclusion or exclusion; Product cognition
operates only on that projection.

The request binds exact organization, subject, purpose, requested scope,
sensitivity, semantic evaluation time, authorization-context revision,
canonical Understanding revision, immutable lineage digest, lineage-policy
version, and material supports. Callers cannot supply a disposition,
authorization result, binding availability result, eligible identifiers, or a
projection revision.

The deterministic result is `eligible` when all required lineage and current
governance are valid, `withheld` when complete governance proves denial, and
`unavailable` when any material branch is missing, malformed, stale, ambiguous,
conflicting, cyclic, unsupported, foreign, or unverifiable. `Unavailable` takes
precedence over `withheld`.

## Revocation and historical Runtime

Every direct and inherited material Source Binding is re-resolved against
current canonical state. Persisted revocation prevents reuse of stale
projections while immutable Theory, Explanation, composition, and ancestry
bytes remain unchanged.

The retained historical Northstar Runtime has no canonical Theory ancestry and
no Explanation governance lineage. It remains byte-identical and is denied
because current eligibility cannot be proven. No automatic migration or
invented lineage is permitted. A future governed reconstruction may create a
successor Runtime through explicit canonical operations.
