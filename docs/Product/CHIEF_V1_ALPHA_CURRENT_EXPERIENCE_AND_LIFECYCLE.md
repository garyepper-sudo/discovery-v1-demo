# Chief V1 Alpha — Current Experience and Lifecycle

**Status:** Current bounded Alpha reference

## Scope and authority

Discovery is a governed organizational-learning platform. Chief V1 is its
first application, and the accepted Alpha wedge is one authorized leader
returning to one recurring consequential Leadership Conversation around one
long-lived `ProductQuestion`.

This document is a concise orientation and does not supersede the governing
documents. When they differ, documentation authority is ordered as follows:

1. Product Constitution;
2. Product Governance;
3. Product Decisions;
4. Canonical Product Architecture;
5. accepted implementation and evidence;
6. roadmap and specifications; then
7. historical planning and prototype material.

## Current experience

Meeting Home and the Meeting Pack are the primary preparation entry. The
visible experience compresses the governed workflow into:

```text
Meeting Home / Meeting Pack → Prepare → Meet → Confirm outcomes → Continue
```

The Meeting Pack is first: concise context and optional private input lead to
Build; supporting analysis remains inspectable through progressive disclosure.
Chief is the visible orchestrator. Counsel, Operator, and Scout are governed
roles over the same substrate, never independent agents, models, memories,
truth stores, or authorization owners.

## Authoritative lifecycle

The internal lifecycle remains distinct from its compressed presentation:

```text
Prepare → Freeze → Capture → Review → Reviewed Carry-Forward → Finalization
→ Closure / What Changed → Prepare Again → distinct successor occurrence
```

Capture creates proposals, not truth. Review and the existing canonical owners
control disposition, admission, and any later Runtime evolution. What Changed
is derived from owner-issued reviewed outcomes. Prepare Again reuses the same
`ProductQuestion` and creates a distinct successor occurrence; it is not a
second occurrence merely because it has been prepared.

Provider analysis is source-scoped and noncanonical. It may produce a validated
candidate for a Meeting Pack only through the existing authorization, lineage,
publication, and owner checks; it does not itself become Evidence,
Organizational Understanding, or an authority grant.

## Trust gates

The required gates are separate and cumulative:

1. authentication;
2. participant identity;
3. organization access;
4. meeting-series access;
5. source access; and
6. current access.

Authorization is evaluated before protected retrieval or body reads. A valid
earlier gate does not imply a later gate, and no UI address, display label, or
provider response grants access.

## Accepted Alpha boundary

AR-6 attempt 90 is the accepted technical Alpha path: its reconciler recorded
63/63 facts and 7/7 measurements, with zero failures and zero blocks, followed
by cleanup and an independent zero check. It exercised real Clerk
authentication, onboarding, organization, and exact-series access; source-
scoped provider-backed analysis; the Meeting Pack; Capture proposals; reviewed
carry-forward; finalization; closure/What Changed; successor preparation;
browser reload and fresh reconstruction; replay; and cleanup.

This evidence establishes only the bounded path above. It does not claim a
completed second meeting, generalized multi-user operation, continuous
monitoring, broad external rollout, production launch, or longitudinal learning
proof. Those remain separate decisions and work.
