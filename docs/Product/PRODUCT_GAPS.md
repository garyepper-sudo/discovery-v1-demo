  replaces compatibility presentation.

`Resolved` status and evidence; it is never silently deleted.

## Audit result

The register consolidates previously identified connector, freshness,
collaboration, permissions, calibration, monitoring, resilience, scale, and
research debt. Existing solved capabilities—durable Question identity,
answer-owned confidence, deterministic workspace projection, abstention,
decision/outcome lineage, model projection, organization isolation, and the
projection firewall—are not listed as unresolved.

## Workflow reconciliation

The binding authority is
[`WORKFLOW_ACCEPTANCE_SPEC.md`](./WORKFLOW_ACCEPTANCE_SPEC.md). Required
pre-wireframe gaps are `GAP-A-005` through `GAP-A-016`, plus existing
`GAP-B-001`, `GAP-B-004`, `GAP-B-010`, and the controlled-test foundation of
`GAP-B-017`. They cover Question, Search, Answer, Confidence, Improve
confidence, Decision, Outcome, Model learns, and New insight in that order.

Existing Beta gaps remain legitimately post-wireframe only where the binding
single-user Google Drive replay does not require them:

- `GAP-B-002`, `GAP-B-003`: additional connectors.
- `GAP-B-005`: semantic freshness beyond source-backed timestamps.
- `GAP-B-006`: connected outcome acquisition; manual outcome completion is
  pre-wireframe under `GAP-A-013`.
- `GAP-B-007`–`GAP-B-009`: portfolio clustering, merge/split, and similarity.
- `GAP-B-011`: production explanation adjudication.
- `GAP-B-012`–`GAP-B-014`: multi-user, multi-perspective, and role-specific
  authorization beyond the exact-user acceptance replay.
- `GAP-B-015`, `GAP-B-016`: broader answer and industry validation.

Production and research gaps retain their existing classifications. Connector
retrieval does not silently promote autonomous reevaluation, notification,
monitoring, enterprise authorization, or experimental cognition.
