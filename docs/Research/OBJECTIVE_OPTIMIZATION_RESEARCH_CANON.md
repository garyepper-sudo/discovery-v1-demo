# Organizational Objective and Optimization Context Research Canon

**Status:** Phase 2D design evidence; benchmark-supported; synthetic only

## Research question

Is `Organizational Understanding + Organizational Objective + Optimization
Context` the smallest sufficient input model for future governed Objective
Recommendations?

## Adopted and adapted principles

| Discipline | Principle | Discovery treatment | Benchmark condition | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decision analysis / value-focused thinking | Values and objectives should precede alternative evaluation; attributes measure objectives rather than replace them. | Adopt: Objective is the prospective end, success criteria are indicators, and meaningful alternatives are mandatory. | Objective-only, no-criterion, no-alternative, metric-substitution, and Goodhart cases. | Synthetic fixtures do not establish stakeholder values in practice. |
| Multi-attribute decision analysis | Multiple objectives require explicit tradeoffs rather than silent aggregation. | Adapt: priority, precedence, constraint designation, or scenario comparison is required; no universal scalar utility is introduced. | Equal- and unequal-authority conflicts, nested scopes, revenue versus margin, cost versus retention. | No quantitative utility elicitation or interpersonal aggregation is validated. |
| Value of information | Information is valuable when it can change a decision, not merely because uncertainty exists. | Adopt: high information value may select an Understanding Recommendation instead of premature business action. | Weak causal support/high information value and strong support/high delay cost. | Benchmark uses ordinal, not monetary, value. |
| Decision quality | A good process and a good outcome are distinct under uncertainty. | Adopt: Objective Recommendation eligibility is judged from available understanding, authority, context, alternatives, and governance; later Outcome does not retroactively validate the process. | Waiting, status quo, irreversible investment, and adverse Outcome controls. | No real decision-maker calibration. |
| Bounded rationality | Organizations satisfice under limited information and conflicting goals. | Adapt: fail-closed thresholds and adaptive questions seek a requisite model, not exhaustive configuration. | Full form versus fixed versus adaptive elicitation. | Does not model politics or search behavior dynamically. |
| Organizational goal conflict / agency | Objective legitimacy depends on authority and stakeholder conflicts cannot be averaged away. | Adopt: authority is separate from epistemic confidence; protected stakeholder harm requires governance or explicit authorization. | Unequal authority, unauthorized objective, distributional harm, contradictory strategy document. | Role and policy semantics remain future authorization implementation work. |
| Contingency theory | Appropriate action depends on evidence-grounded organizational and environmental conditions. | Adapt: crisis, growth, regulation, maturity, capacity, and volatility stay in Organizational Understanding and are referenced by context/constraints. | Same objective under routine, crisis, regulation, capacity, and volatility conditions. | No claim of universal organization–environment fit. |
| Exploration versus exploitation | Learning and near-term execution can compete. | Adapt: `maximize-learning` is an Optimization Context mode, while learning remains a secondary effect for an Objective Recommendation. | High learning value and high cost-of-delay cases. | Does not create an ambidexterity object or optimizer. |
| Dynamic capabilities | Sensing, seizing, and reconfiguring depend on actual organizational capacity. | Defer as a named theory; use existing evidence-grounded capacity and change-readiness facts. | High/low execution capacity. | No new capability primitive or causal claim. |
| Control and systems theory | State, target, control action, observability, delays, and disturbances are distinct. | Adopt boundary: Understanding describes state; Objective describes target; Recommendation proposes control; Outcome supplies feedback; delay and volatility affect context. | Time horizon, delay, crisis, volatility, and Outcome-wait cases. | No control-system model or stability proof is introduced. |
| Real options | Irreversibility and uncertainty can make delay or a reversible pilot valuable. | Adopt: reversibility and preservation of optionality are explicit context/constraint concerns; status quo and delay are valid alternatives. | Irreversible investment, reversible pilot, and waiting cases. | No financial option valuation. |
| Risk governance | Risk appetite is set by authorized governance; risk capacity and exposure are contextual facts. | Adapt: preference and capacity remain distinct, governance rules cannot be overridden, and high-stakes action needs confirmed authority. | Risk-posture, liquidity, regulatory, and protected-harm cases. | No enterprise risk taxonomy or numeric risk model. |
| Goodhart-style metric failure | Direct optimization of a proxy can degrade the underlying objective. | Adopt: metrics remain success criteria; metric/objective conflict fails closed. | Metric target versus Objective and proxy-gaming negative controls. | The benchmark detects declared fixture conflict, not emergent gaming. |

## Classification

- **Adopt directly:** objective/metric separation, alternatives, authority versus confidence, immutable correction/supersession, status quo/delay/learning alternatives, governance fail-closed behavior.
- **Adapt:** multi-objective tradeoffs, ordinal value of information, risk appetite/capacity separation, adaptive elicitation, exploration/exploitation, real-options reasoning.
- **Benchmark before adoption:** inherited defaults, high-confidence inferred Objectives, nested scope precedence, distributional tradeoff authorization.
- **Defer:** quantitative utility functions, numeric forecasts, automatic stakeholder aggregation, monetary value of information, autonomous optimization.
- **Reject:** silent metric substitution, universal optimization defaults, authority inferred from confidence, a separate Operating Context object without incremental evidence.

## Result boundary

The research justifies contract design and deterministic synthetic comparison.
It does not justify Objective Recommendation generation, persistence, numeric
utility, Production activation, or claims of real organizational validity.
