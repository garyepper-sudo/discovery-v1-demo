# Organizational Understanding Gate 1 V1

Status: benchmark-only research for `GAP-D-004`; no architecture change and no Production Product path.

This harness exercises three independently authored synthetic worlds across content grounding (B1), chronology and temporal state (B2), and contradiction/origin handling (B3). It reuses Discovery vocabulary for governed source identity/version, permission-scoped packet composition, evidence provenance, temporal applicability, entity continuity, epistemic status, and contradiction representation without reading Runtime, a database, or Product transport.

Each world produces four fresh-context candidate packets: baseline, a relevant source change with one withheld source, temporal-state variation, and contradiction/origin variation. The candidate receives only generic source labels, numbered authorized passages, governed temporal context, the question, and one invariant instruction. Internal packet identifiers and variants are not part of the model request. The deterministic extractive baseline contains passage references, known-time ordering, explicit conflict passages, and commitment passages only; it does not produce mechanisms or executive conclusions.

Frozen hidden keys, intervention expectations, and scoring thresholds remain outside this repository and outside every candidate request. Candidate output is imported only from a private task-owned location and structurally validated for citation closure to the exact authorized packet.

Independent content-safe evaluation completed for 12 candidate responses across the three synthetic worlds. The sealed evaluator accepted Gate 1 V1: B1 grounding, B2 chronology/temporal state, B3 contradiction/disagreement, and selective intervention all passed. Aggregate citation support was 0.97; the evaluation recorded no material unsupported claims, withheld-source leakage, future-information contamination, historical overwrite, invented reconciliation, duplicate-origin confidence inflation, or seniority-as-authority. This result is bounded to the frozen synthetic worlds and does not establish causal-mechanism identification, learning, or general organizational understanding.

The implementation addresses research measurement debt, not a registered Product capability: `GAP-D-004`, roadmap benchmark research/longitudinal-understanding sequence. Existing owners are reused only as vocabulary and contract precedents. Out of scope: Runtime mutation, source admission, Product contracts, migrations, live provider transport, and architecture changes.

The strongest possible future claim is bounded to the three frozen synthetic worlds and only after independent evaluation of actual outputs. It cannot establish general organizational understanding.
