# Existing Benchmark Audit for Discovery Intelligence

**Audit date:** 2026-07-28
**Scope:** Current `engine/benchmark/**`, product validation commands, and canonical product benchmark
**Method:** Repository inventory and contract review; existing behavior was not changed

## Finding

Discovery has extensive evidence for accuracy, determinism, provenance,
architecture, causal discipline, decision integrity, communication contracts,
and longitudinal mechanics. It does not yet evaluate improvement in
organizational understanding as one explicit outcome.

Understanding quality is currently inferred from:

- successful production of cognitive layers;
- expected mechanism, capability, and theory recovery;
- executive-understanding proxy scores;
- provenance and confidence checks;
- decision and recommendation quality;
- learning and longitudinal stability;
- Product Translation and Truthful Utility negative controls.

Those are necessary contributors. None alone proves that a person acquired
clearer, more useful, truthful organizational understanding.

## Gaps closed by the framework

The Discovery Intelligence Scorecard adds:

- direct dimension ownership and weights;
- explicit human-only boundaries;
- decision utility independent of correctness;
- Evidence acquisition quality and recovery after insufficiency;
- executive and non-expert communication review;
- one longitudinal understanding-improvement contract;
- a domain-balanced, organization-isolated corpus;
- deterministic reporting with confidence and regression disclosure.

## Gaps intentionally not claimed as closed

- Human judgment is not automated.
- Synthetic scenarios do not prove design-partner value.
- Estimated information gain is not observed business impact.
- Public corpus performance does not establish generalization.
- A score does not authorize cognitive or architectural promotion.

## Reuse rule

Future adapters may import immutable results from existing benchmark runs into
the appropriate measured metrics. They must preserve source identity,
framework version, organization scope, and the original benchmark's meaning.
They must not rewrite existing suites or reinterpret a passing subsystem check
as direct human understanding.
