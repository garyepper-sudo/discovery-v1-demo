# Candidate Enriched Mechanism Shadow Experiment 001

## Executive summary

**Classification: D — Existing Artifacts Insufficient.**

The candidate representation can be populated safely enough to improve
auditability, but not qualification. Across 15 production Mechanisms:

- upstream drivers were explicit in 15/15;
- downstream endpoints were explicit in 15/15;
- mediation was explicit or deterministically derivable in 0/15;
- activation was derivable in 1/15;
- persistence was unavailable in 15/15;
- implications were unavailable in 15/15;
- falsification was unavailable in 15/15;
- generated Contradictions could be retained only as ambiguous competing
  context, not deterministic discrimination.

No enriched contract improved precision while preserving recall. Existing
artifacts therefore do not support a qualified, discriminating, falsifiable
Mechanism without invention.

## Production input audit

Every scenario entered unchanged production through `runDiscoveryV3()` and
`evolveOrganizationRuntime()`. Enrichment consumed generated cognition only.
Expected chains, mediation, activation, implications, interventions, scoring
truth, and future outcomes were excluded until registration completed.

All candidate statements preserve artifact IDs, raw Evidence IDs where
available, production stage, fixed derivation rule, and derivation status.

## Scenario candidates and positive preservation

All six positives preserved their generated cause/effect relationships,
confidence, silos, and Evidence lineage as fragments or hypotheses. None
became qualified.

- Commercial and escalation candidates retained useful cross-silo endpoints
  but lacked mediation and downstream implication.
- Local optimization retained adjacent cost/lead-time fragments rather than
  the complete system chain.
- Customer misdiagnosis retained workflow/training and awareness fragments
  without discriminating them.
- Delayed consequence lacked a canonical supported horizon.
- Contradictory local explanations preserved contradictions as ambiguity, not
  adjudicated alternatives.

The representation honestly recommends the smallest missing Evidence:
mediation between endpoints, activation/persistence conditions, evidence that
discriminates alternatives, a future implication, and falsification evidence.

## False-positive suppression

The prior insufficient-evidence Mechanism was reproduced under current
production eligibility. Its conservative candidate reports missing mediation,
activation, alternatives, implication, and falsification. Mediated and
qualified contracts reject it for structural reasons, but also reject every
positive because the same fields are absent there.

The key suppressive fields are mediation plus implication/discrimination—not
confidence. They cannot yet be populated safely.

## Recoverability

Prediction recoverability was partial at best: endpoints were available, but a
conditional trigger, supported horizon, complete chain, and falsification were
not. No fully recoverable scenario-specific implication was produced.

Intervention leverage was unavailable or partial only where a non-generic
mediating string existed. No candidate supplied an intervention class,
observable success signal, and falsification signal without adding facts.

Direct Prediction or Intervention ancestry is therefore not justified.

## Field ablations and counterfactuals

- Removing lineage eliminates trust even when endpoint prose remains.
- Removing the bridge can remove or fragment generated structure.
- Restoring complementary Evidence recovers only structure production already
  generated.
- Redundant Evidence can raise confidence without adding completeness.
- Low-quality conflict adds ambiguity but does not create deterministic
  discrimination.
- Reversed time does not reliably weaken production causal direction.
- Removing recommended-next-Evidence guidance changes usability, not
  qualification.

Mediation, activation, implication, falsification, and lineage are essential
for qualification. Recommended next Evidence is helpful but not a qualification
field. Persistence and alternatives are rarely safely derivable. No tested
field is redundant when genuinely present; most are simply unavailable.

## Minimal safe representation

The existing artifacts safely support only:

- source Mechanism identity;
- explicit upstream statement;
- explicit downstream statement;
- confidence;
- supporting silos;
- supporting artifacts and raw Evidence;
- ambiguous contradiction context;
- explicit missing-field inventory;
- missing-field-derived next-Evidence requests;
- classification as fragment or provisional hypothesis.

They do not support `qualified` status.

## Direct answers

1. Mediation cannot be derived safely.
2. Activation is derivable in only one case; persistence is unavailable.
3. Contradictions identify ambiguous competing context, not discriminated
   alternatives.
4. Falsification cannot be derived.
5. Discriminating implications cannot be derived.
6. Upstream and downstream endpoint statements are mostly explicit.
7. Activation is rarely deterministic; no other missing causal field is.
8. Mediation, persistence, implications, and falsification are unavailable;
   alternatives are ambiguous.
9. Mediation and discriminating implication most suppress false positives.
10. Those same fields reduce recall to zero because production does not
    represent them.
11. Enriched qualification does not outperform production.
12. It preserves positive relationships as fragments or hypotheses, not
    qualified Mechanisms.
13. It rejects the insufficient case only under contracts that reject every
    positive.
14. No complete Prediction implication is reconstructable without facts.
15. No complete intervention leverage target is reconstructable without facts.
16. Request the first missing causal relationship, activation evidence,
    alternative-discriminating evidence, future implication, or falsification
    evidence—not generic additional documents.
17. One audit representation works across families, but none can be qualified.
18. The minimal safe representation is explicit endpoints, lineage, confidence,
    ambiguity, missingness, and targeted next-Evidence guidance.
19. The next justified step is **further benchmark research**, specifically a
    read-only producer audit for mediation and implication source availability.
20. Before production changes, demonstrate safe field population, improved
    precision, nonzero recall, false-positive reduction, counterfactual
    sensitivity, and useful prediction/intervention recoverability.

## Architectural recommendation

Do not propose a production Mechanism type yet. Do not add a qualification
score or downstream ancestry. Continue benchmark research at the existing
Observation/Signal/Theme/Mechanism producers to determine whether mediation and
implication are generated transiently and lost, or never generated at all.
