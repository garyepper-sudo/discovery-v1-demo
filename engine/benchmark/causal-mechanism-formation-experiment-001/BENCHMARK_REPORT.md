# Causal Mechanism Formation Experiment 001 — Benchmark Report

## Executive Summary

Classification:

```text
A — Qualified Causal Formation Demonstrated
```

Within the intentionally narrow explicit-relationship boundary, conservative
formation achieved `1.00` precision and `0.75` recall: six of eight positive
families qualified and all nine controls remained unqualified. This is not
evidence for immediate production adoption. Branching and converging formation
failed, and the fixture isolates formation by making local causal relationships
explicit in raw Evidence.

## Production Input Audit

The exact entry point was `runDiscoveryV3` with raw
`InvestigationEvidenceSource[]`, followed by in-memory
`evolveOrganizationRuntime`. The run consumed raw Evidence and generated
Observations, Signals, Contradictions, Themes, Phenomena, Mechanisms, Beliefs,
Concepts, Theories, Conditions, and Organizational State. The adapter added
stable raw-Evidence artifact identities, not causal meaning.

Production reaches cause/mechanism/effect summaries with Evidence and silo
lineage. It stops before explicit multi-hop mediation, activation, comparative
alternatives, conditional implications, and falsification.

## False-Positive Analysis

The prior quality-review pattern remained unqualified. It contains neither an
explicit directed relationship chain nor a mediator, activation condition,
discriminating alternative comparison, implication, or falsification
criterion. The request is for Evidence linking review-induced caution to an
intermediate behavior and then to an outcome, plus Evidence testing a credible
alternative. Suppression is structural, not confidence-threshold based.

## Positive Preservation

Six linear or conditional positive families qualified. Branching and converging
cases remained hypotheses, preserving useful recall without pretending that a
single maximal path represents multi-branch topology.

## Alternative Discrimination

Alternatives were read only from Evidence statements using one fixed rule.
Conservative qualification required an alternative but did not claim a
fully calibrated comparative probability. The experiment demonstrates
structural alternative awareness; relative-support calibration remains a gap.

## Held-Out Implications

Implications were registered before future Evidence was read. The registered
trigger/outcome pairs aligned with held-out outcomes in the qualified cases and
the controls did not gain unsupported implications. They outperform
Organizational State and generic summary on explicit conditional structure, but
the benchmark does not establish production prediction ancestry.

## Intervention-Leverage Audit

Qualified chains exposed a link, expected downstream signal, and a criterion
that would weaken the intervention theory. Leverage was fully recoverable for
qualified linear cases and partial or unavailable for incomplete cases. No
production Intervention was generated.

## Field Availability

Upstream drivers, downstream outcomes, and activation conditions were most
often supportable from explicit relationship Evidence. Mediation was available
for multi-hop chains. Persistence was not separately supportable and remained
empty. Alternatives were available only when Evidence contained a credible
alternative. Implications and falsification were deterministically derived from
complete chains, with full lineage.

## Counterfactuals

- Bridge and mediator removal broke complete paths and reduced qualification.
- Activation removal prevented a supportable condition.
- Alternative strengthening did not inflate causal completeness.
- Redundancy added records but not structural completeness.
- Complementarity preserved or completed a relationship path.
- Temporal reversal rejected direction.
- Lineage removal prevented qualification.
- Realistic noise did not change the registered chain.

## Determinism and leakage

Repeated execution, reversed scenario order, reversed Evidence order, and
reversed silo order were byte-identical. Production artifacts, candidates,
alternatives, implications, falsification, next-Evidence guidance,
counterfactuals, and `RESULTS.json` were stable.

Opaque case IDs carry no answers. Producer modules contain no case lookup,
scoring-truth import, held-out future import, or scenario-specific template.
Every populated causal field traces to Evidence and a benchmark-local artifact
identity.

## Minimal Safe Producer Contract

The evidence supports only this contract:

> Given explicit directed relationship Evidence, join exact adjacent endpoints
> into deterministic paths; preserve full lineage; derive conditions only from
> stated relationship conditions; consider only Evidence-grounded alternatives;
> qualify only a multi-hop, multi-silo path with a condition, implication, and
> falsification criterion; otherwise remain provisional and request the
> smallest missing relationship.

It does not yet support implicit relation extraction, synonym resolution,
branching/converging topology, calibrated alternative adjudication, or separate
persistence inference.

## Architectural Recommendation

**Benchmark-only candidate producer refinement.** Do not propose a production
Mechanism type or downstream ancestry yet. The next experiment should test the
same contract on implicit wording and explicitly model branching/converging
graphs without scenario-specific rules.
