# Benchmark Report

## Executive summary

**Classification: B — Partial Capability.**

Unchanged production cognition generated a qualifying cross-silo Mechanism in
four of six positive scenarios. That is genuine production-derived structure.
It did not demonstrate end-to-end emergent organizational intelligence:
production combined scored **48.868160**, below the generic all-Evidence
summary at **56.909085**, and failed decisive prediction and intervention
gates. It also invented a qualifying mechanism for one insufficient-evidence
negative control.

The prior result therefore narrows rather than disappears: production can form
some cross-silo structure, but it does not yet reliably turn that structure
into superior, discriminating organizational understanding.

## Production path audit

Raw evidence entered `runDiscoveryV3()` unchanged except for removal of the
fixture-only silo label. Production generated all primary cognition. The result
then entered `evolveOrganizationRuntime()` against a fresh in-memory Runtime
under a fixed clock. No stage received scoring truth or future Evidence.

The full stage list and exact functions are machine-recorded in
`RESULTS.json`. No production stage was simulated. The benchmark-local
selection adapter requires at least three source silos and rejects a mechanism
whose full material wording appears in one source. The verbal adapter changes
presentation only.

## Representative verbal artifact

For scenario 001, the strongest production-combined claim was:

> Enterprise agreements increasingly include customer-specific workflow
> commitments; Discovery sees one condition depending on another enabling
> condition; roadmap exceptions for customer-specific workflows continue to
> increase.

The projected version explains that Sales, Product, and Operations each see a
different portion of the pattern and lists those source-linked observations.
It predicts continued roadmap exceptions if enterprise customization
continues, states falsification conditions, and carries the existing production
recommendation. It is clearer and traceable, but it omits Finance and does not
reach the expected margin-erosion mechanism. This is projection value, not
proof of stronger cognition.

## Negative controls

Four of five controls abstained. Scenario 009 did not: production connected a
single quality review, added approvals, and manager caution into a qualifying
cross-silo mechanism despite the evidence being intentionally
underdetermined. Negative-control abstention therefore failed.

## Ablations

Removing silos caused abstention in **78.5714%** of tested removals, establishing
counterfactual sensitivity but not semantic correctness. Mechanism removal and
lineage removal eliminated the benchmark-selected combined output. Removing
Contradictions, history, Theories, Conditions, or Organizational State after
generation did not change that output because the current selection boundary
reads the generated Mechanism directly.

In scenario 001:

- removing complementary Evidence or collapsing all documents into one silo
  eliminated the qualifying mechanism;
- adding irrelevant noise or a low-reliability conflict did not eliminate it;
- adding repeated Evidence increased confidence;
- increasing source count without semantic diversity also increased
  confidence.

The last two findings are warnings: evidence volume still affects confidence
without demonstrating greater relational complementarity.

## Preconditions analysis

The four successful positive formations and two failed formations had the same
mean Evidence count (**4.5**) and mean silo diversity (**4.5**). Raw quantity
and diversity alone did not separate success from failure. The immediate
separator was whether production actually generated a cross-silo Mechanism
(mean **1.0** versus **0.0**), but that is a proximate output, not a causal
precondition.

Candidate hypotheses for Experiment 003:

- relational complementarity matters more than volume;
- diversity matters only when lineage spans distinct contributing facts;
- temporal depth is family-specific;
- contradictions help only when resolved into a discriminating relationship;
- lineage predicts groundedness more directly than correctness;
- noise can lower observability even while increasing Evidence count.

These are descriptive hypotheses, not universal thresholds.

## Leakage and determinism

All nine programmatic leakage checks passed. Scenario and source identifiers
are neutral; scoring fields are absent from inference; futures are withheld
until after registration; the generic baseline receives all raw Evidence; and
all non-projection adapters are shape-only.

Repeated execution, reversed scenario order, reversed raw Evidence order,
reversed silo order, reversed within-silo order, registered predictions, verbal
outputs, and the machine-readable result were stable. Canonical ordering occurs
at the raw Evidence entry adapter before unchanged production cognition.

## Gate results

Passed: raw-Evidence origin, counterfactual necessity, determinism, fair
information access, and no semantic injection.

Failed: cross-silo formation across all positives, individual
non-recoverability, generic-summary superiority, held-out prediction
improvement, intervention improvement, complete positive-case lineage, and
negative-control abstention.

Because decisive gates 2, 4, 5, and 6 failed, the end-to-end claim is not
demonstrated.

    and intervention, and permission-safe de-identified production-derived
    replay.

## Scientific limitations and decision

This is a deterministic synthetic benchmark with explicit post-registration
semantic scoring, not external organizational evidence. The generic baseline
is extractive rather than provider-backed. Ablations cannot establish causal
preconditions.

Do not authorize a new cognition layer or external claim. The minimum next
step is a benchmark-only producer trace comparing the two failed positive cases
and the insufficient-evidence false positive at Observation → Signal → Theme →
Mechanism boundaries. Production should remain unchanged.
