# Independent Natural Language Generalization Experiment 003 — Report

## Executive Summary

The experiment answers the final question conservatively:

> **Discovery has demonstrated a benchmark-specific causal-formation
> capability. A generalizable organizational causal reasoning capability has
> not been demonstrated.**

The unchanged Experiment 002 producer passed its original controlled regression
but qualified none of 24 independently phrased positive scenarios. All nine
negative controls remained rejected.

## Primary Results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Controlled Experiment 002 regression | PASS |
| Natural-language positive qualifications | 0/24 |
| Natural-language negative qualifications | 0/9 |
| Natural-language recall | 0.00 |
| False positives | 0 |
| False negatives | 24 |
| Terminology invariance | 0.00 |

Precision is reported as `0` by the benchmark’s zero-denominator convention
because the producer made no positive qualifications. This is abstention, not
evidence of high precision.

## Failure Analysis

The producer recognizes only the bounded sentence forms used in Experiment 002:

- `Evidence indicates that … leads to … when …`
- `After … changed, … changed in the following period`
- fixed intervention-response phrasing;
- fixed comparable-group phrasing;
- fixed repeated-transition phrasing.

The new corpus preserved equivalent organizational realities without those
surface forms. No causal edges entered graph composition, so topology,
mediation, alternatives, implications, and falsification could not recover.

Observed failure phenomena included:

- synonym and terminology drift;
- implicit mediation;
- hedging;
- political attribution;
- passive voice;
- pronoun reference;
- shorthand and fragmented structure;
- missing or indirect chronology;
- document-style variation.

The universal failure across styles indicates exact-grammar dependence is the
earliest responsible limitation. It is not evidence that production cognition
fabricated causal claims.

## Writing-Style Analysis

Recovery was `0%` for all ten styles: board summaries, customer interviews,
engineering standups, executive emails, finance commentary, fragmented bullets,
HR observations, meeting notes, operational reports, and Slack.

## Industry Analysis

Recovery was `0%` across Banking, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing,
Professional Services, and Software. Because no language variant recovered,
industry invariance was not demonstrated.

## Terminology Analysis

None of 24 terminology sets recovered. Equivalent ideas expressed as special
handling, clinical variants, engineered orders, ministerial clearance,
purchase-price variance, workflow completion, priority resets, and
demand-capacity gaps did not enter causal edge formation.

## Confidence Calibration

Mean candidate confidence was approximately `0.30` for both positive and
negative natural-language scenarios. The producer correctly remained cautious,
but its confidence did not discriminate recoverable positives from controls.

## Baseline Comparison

- **Best individual silo:** no silo contained the complete distributed topology.
- **Generic summarization:** could preserve narrative content but has no
  grounded edge-basis or qualification contract.
- **Full Canonical Combined cognition:** production generated artifacts were
  preserved and measured, but the unchanged candidate parser did not translate
  their varied language into topology.

No baseline was intentionally weakened. No baseline result justifies claiming
general causal formation.

## Stress and Determinism

Repeated execution, reversed scenario order, reversed Evidence order, and
reversed silo order were byte-identical. Candidate formation and production
artifact counts remained stable. The failure is deterministic.

## Leakage and Producer Integrity

- The Experiment 002 producer was imported unchanged.
- Its SHA-256 is recorded in `RESULTS.json`.
- It imports no Experiment 003 corpus or truth.
- Scenario IDs are opaque.
- Scoring truth is not passed into registration.
- The independent corpus does not use the controlled lead phrases.
- No thresholds, heuristics, confidence, or qualification rules changed.

## Scientific Conclusion

Experiment 002 demonstrated that deterministic graph composition and strict
qualification work when causal relations have already been normalized into a
small controlled grammar. Experiment 003 shows that the same producer does not
generalize that capability to varied organizational language.

The result should not be described as organizational causal understanding. It
is a benchmark-specific formation capability over pre-normalized linguistic
structure.

Production adoption is not recommended. The producer must remain unchanged in
this experiment, so the appropriate outcome is diagnosis only—not a proposed
fix or downstream ancestry work.
