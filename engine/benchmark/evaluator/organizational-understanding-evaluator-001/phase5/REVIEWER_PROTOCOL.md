# Phase 5 Blinded Reviewer Protocol

## Eligibility and independence

Reviewers must be able to distinguish factual, inferential, causal,
predictive, and uncertain claims; complete training and qualification; attest
independence and conflicts; and confirm that they have not accessed answer keys
or other reviewers' responses. Discovery expertise is neither required nor
preferred.

Each confirmatory item requires at least two frozen independent responses. A
material disagreement requires an independent third reviewer or blinded
consensus. Reviewers may not communicate before their submissions are frozen,
and no reviewer may resolve their own dispute alone.

## Reviewer task

Compare neutral Proposition A and Proposition B using only the supplied packet
and frozen rubric. Record classification, meaning agreement, polarity,
modality, temporality, applicable causal and contradiction fields, confidence
and lineage agreement, reviewer confidence, escalation need, and a concise
structured justification. Use `ambiguous` when the packet is insufficient;
do not infer missing facts.

## Blinding

Do not seek or record treatment identity, Discovery origin, aggregate scores,
other treatment outputs, expected winners, commercial implications, prior
judgments, or answer keys. Candidate retrieval rank is intentionally absent.

## Training and qualification

Training covers the ten classifications, ten proposition families, causal and
temporal overclaim, polarity reversal, unsupported versus irrelevant,
ambiguity, missing claims, duplicates, and confidence separation. Training and
qualification packets are excluded from study evidence. Qualification requires
all integrity questions and at least 80% of frozen classification controls.

## Disagreements

Original responses remain immutable. Exact/equivalent and other preregistered
adjacent boundaries may be analyzed as adjacent but are still reported exactly.
Material disagreements require escalation. Rubric-defect-affected items remain
research evidence but cannot enter the confirmatory gold set.
