# Material Information Acquisition Calibration Experiment 001 — Report

## Classification

**B — Selector promising but live shadow calibration required.**

The calibrated ordinal treatment selected the designated action in **100% of 24 held-out synthetic scenarios**, compared with **70.8%** for the current Phase 2C baseline, with zero governance or authorization violations. Its stopping precision and recall were both **100%**. That supports the contract structure, but not production implementation: organizational value, actual burden, generalized reliability, cost, and delay lack real action-level counterfactual Outcomes.

## Interpretation

- Hard gates, material-effect stopping, dominance, ties, and sequential recomputation are stable in fixtures.
- Expected information and discrimination are partially sourceable now from canonical opportunities and Unknowns.
- Organizational relevance is Question-relative for understanding work; Objective or Decision relevance requires confirmed governed context.
- Burden, cost, delay, reliability, and reversibility remain action-owner estimates until Outcome-backed calibration exists.
- One universal stopping threshold is rejected. The governed combination uses sufficiency, material effect, reliability, and burden within exact context.
- No universal utility scalar is required.
- This is benchmark performance, not a production-performance claim.

## Readiness boundary

Do not implement the production selector yet. Run read-only live shadow calibration in which the selector predicts, a user or existing workflow independently chooses, the existing owner executes, and actual receipts, Evidence, Unknown, Answer, and downstream eligibility changes are compared.
