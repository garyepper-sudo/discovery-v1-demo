# Material Information Acquisition Experiment 001 — Report

## Result

The governed hybrid is the bounded benchmark winner. It evaluates one common candidate-action contract while preserving meaningful differences among asking, searching, comparing, requesting, measuring, surveying, experimenting, waiting, stopping, and abstaining.

Expected information gain alone is insufficient: it selects unauthorized, unreliable, disproportionately costly, or decision-irrelevant acquisition in negative controls. Expected organizational value alone is also insufficient because value without authority, reliability, or cost can be unsafe. Fixed workflows impose avoidable burden and miss domain-specific action value.

## General algorithm

1. Identify the uncertainty that most materially limits current Organizational Understanding or a governed downstream choice.
2. Enumerate currently available acquisition actions from existing owners.
3. Reject unauthorized or governance-prohibited actions.
4. Estimate bounded information gain, organizational value, burden, cost, delay, reliability, evidence quality, and reversibility.
5. Select exactly one action using deterministic ordering.
6. Choose stopping or abstention when net material value is insufficient.
7. Admit resulting information only through existing canonical controls, update Organizational Understanding, and repeat.

The benchmark does not justify a universal numeric production utility function. Its weighted score is an experimental comparison device.

## Limits

The scenarios and values are authored. They establish architectural discrimination, not calibrated real-world utility, causal effect, monetary value, or autonomous acquisition safety. Live validation and contract design remain required.
