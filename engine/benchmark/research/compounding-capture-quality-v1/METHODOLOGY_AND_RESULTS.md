# Compounding and Capture-Quality V1 — Methodology and Results

> **SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK — NOT CUSTOMER OR PRODUCTION EVIDENCE**

## Product hypothesis and claim boundary

Reviewed, status-aware prior state may preserve more relevant organizational context per unit of active context across recurring cycles. This deterministic existence test measures packet structure—not model output, user behavior, customer value, or organizational performance.

## Design

Three synthetic scenarios × five frozen seeds × eight cycles × two budgets compare A current authorized raw sources, B accumulated authorized raw history with fair truncation, C the same current facts plus prior-cycle reviewed state, and D the same current facts plus an unreviewed naive summary. C is tested at 100%, 95%, 90%, 80%, and stale/systematically biased review. Hidden truth has effective intervals, chronology, authority, permission, relevance, future outcomes, rejected proposals, corrections, contradictions, noise, and duplicate sources. It is serialized only to grader packets.

Metrics were frozen before generation. Estimated tokens are `ceil(UTF-8 bytes / 4)`. Rows are paired by scenario, seed, cycle, budget, and quality. Means, medians, sample standard deviations, centered-cycle OLS slopes, cycle-by-condition interactions, and a deterministic 5,000-resample scenario×seed cluster bootstrap with type-7 percentile intervals are reported. No opaque composite Discovery score is used.

Graph 1 uses the preregistered 2,048-token, 100%-review-quality slice. Its statistical summary includes 32 cycle-level coverage intervals (four conditions × eight cycles), each calculated directly from 15 distinct scenario × seed observations with the same deterministic 5,000-resample cluster bootstrap and 95% type-7 percentile convention. All 32 observed intervals collapse to the mean because every unit within each cell has the same coverage value. This is genuine deterministic agreement—and metric saturation—within this synthetic slice, not evidence of certainty or robustness under authentic organizational heterogeneity. Graph 1 is suitable as a methodology illustration only.

## Results and falsification controls

The generated corpus classifies 100%-correct reviewed state as **Structural compounding supported**. Active-context efficiency versus full history is **Inconclusive** under the frozen rule. Stale/biased review creates structural harm and is preserved in data and charts; lower review accuracy reduces the advantage. The break-even statement is limited to discrete tested levels. Scenario/seed uncertainty is not suppressed. All conditions share byte-identical current-cycle facts; future, rejected, and permission-restricted items are prohibited from model packets; deterministic replay is byte-identical.

These outcomes are generator-defined structural evidence, not an empirical estimate. Model-mediated quality compounding: **NOT TESTED**. Authentic customer compounding: **NOT TESTED**. Organizational outcomes: **NOT TESTED**.

## Threats and next evidence

The simulation may favor its status representation, uses estimated rather than provider tokens, and cannot measure comprehension, useful correction, meeting readiness, behavior, or outcomes. A separately authorized blinded model benchmark must execute frozen randomized packets under one model/prompt and grade against hidden truth. Design-partner evidence then requires consented authentic cycles, privacy-safe instrumentation, blinded human adjudication, and outcome-specific protocols.

Permitted wording: “In a controlled synthetic longitudinal benchmark, Discovery’s reviewed-state representation retained more relevant current organizational context per unit of active context than full accumulated raw history.” Prohibited wording includes “Discovery beats frontier AI,” “Discovery makes organizations X% smarter,” customer-improvement percentages, or “the compounding moat is proven.” No chart may appear without its synthetic label and methodology metadata.
