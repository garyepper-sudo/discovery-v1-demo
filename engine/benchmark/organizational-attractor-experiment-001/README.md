# Organizational Attractor Experiment 001

## Status

Benchmark-only research experiment. Nothing in this directory defines
production cognition, Runtime, schema, capability, API, or application
behavior.

## Research question

Can a deterministic, falsifiable representation of a persistent
organizational direction add predictive value beyond:

1. carrying the currently observed Organizational State forward; and
2. extrapolating the mechanisms active in the latest period?

The experiment does not assume that an organization has consciousness or
desire. “Attractor” means an inferred system-level tendency produced by
distributed structures, incentives, information flows, decisions, and
restoring mechanisms.

## Experimental boundary

The fixture adapter supplies experimental semantic observations:

- direction;
- strength;
- whether an observation is structural or behavioral;
- mechanism;
- period;
- activating condition;
- independent source identity.

No observation says that an attractor exists or names the inferred target
state. The adapter is nevertheless stronger than raw production Evidence. Its
labels are benchmark-owned and are a construct-validity limitation, not a
production contract.

The experiment does not invoke or modify Runtime. It does not write to a
fixture. It does not add a package script.

## Synthetic organization

The primary organization publicly favors local autonomy while five independent
sources show:

- repeated executive overrides;
- managerial risk avoidance;
- incentives that penalize visible mistakes;
- concentrated decision information;
- approval dependence under ambiguity.

A delegation intervention creates genuine short-term decentralization.

The fixture then branches:

- **Restoration A:** structural mechanisms remain and centralization returns.
- **Restoration B:** incentives, information distribution, and executive
  practice change; decentralization persists.

## Candidate inference

The experimental inference adapter:

1. sorts Evidence by stable identity;
2. bounds each observation's contribution;
3. distinguishes structural from behavioral observations;
4. compares centralizing and decentralizing support;
5. requires three independent sources;
6. requires two restoring mechanisms;
7. requires observations across two periods;
8. requires two structural observations;
9. abstains when directional balance is below `0.28`;
10. retains exact supporting and opposing Evidence identities.

The thresholds are experimental constants. They are not proposed production
confidence formulas.

## Prediction models

### Model 1 — Organizational State

Carries the observed post-intervention state through all horizons.

### Model 2 — Mechanisms

Carries the net direction of mechanisms observed in the latest period through
all horizons.

### Model 3 — Candidate Attractor

Predicts a short transition, restoration over medium and long horizons when
the underlying structure remains, and durable change when at least three
opposing structural mechanisms change.

These are controlled comparison adapters, not claims that production
Organizational State or Mechanisms currently implement these exact forecast
policies.

## Negative controls

The experiment penalizes a false attractor by 30 points and fails its
negative-control hard gate. Controls cover:

- no stable direction;
- balanced contradictory structure;
- genuine directional change without demonstrated persistence;
- insufficient Evidence.

## Scoring rubric

| Dimension | Points | Objective requirement |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Emergence | 15 | At least three independent and heterogeneous sources; no explicit attractor statement |
| Mechanistic grounding | 15 | At least three restoring mechanisms with exact Evidence identity |
| Persistence | 15 | Temporary change and restoration are distinguished |
| Intervention sensitivity | 15 | Behavioral change lowers confidence; structural change removes or reverses the candidate |
| Prediction quality | 20 | Attractor accuracy exceeds both comparison models |
| Falsifiability | 10 | At least three observable invalidation criteria |
| Counter-Evidence integration | 5 | Opposing Evidence is retained and lowers confidence |
| Determinism | 5 | Repeated and reversed-order outputs are byte-equal |

Every hard gate must pass:

- distributed emergence;
- zero negative-control false positives;
- incremental prediction value;
- falsifiability;
- intervention sensitivity;
- determinism.

## Run

```bash
npx tsx engine/benchmark/organizational-attractor-experiment-001/runOrganizationalAttractorExperiment001.ts
```

The runner asserts all hard gates and prints the complete trace and score.

## Promotion boundary

A passing result authorizes only further research. Production consideration
would require a held-out benchmark that obtains its semantic inputs from the
unchanged production cognition chain, compares against full production
Organizational State and Mechanism outputs, and proves that the value is not
merely temporal composition of existing objects.
